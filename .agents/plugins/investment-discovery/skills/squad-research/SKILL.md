2. Explore existing historical sources within allowed investments and resource ceilings. Retain mechanism, target/duration, original text/URL, publication/revision and availability evidence, confounders, contrary findings and limits.
3. Ingest dated research through [knowledge](references/knowledge.md); verify retrieval. Delegated researchers return batches to owner.
4. Follow [one-shot historical method](references/methodology.md): develop on earlier data, freeze plan before seeing later test outcomes, run one test, preserve rejection and move on after failure. Related new premise follows exact input/formula hatch. Explicitly prohibit p-fishing and related selection practices.
5. Historically qualified ideas support further investigation and genuine future predictions through [commands](../squad-onboard/references/commands.md#research). [Live validation](../squad-monitor/references/validation.md) owns endpoints and 30-day trial. Apply shared [backup procedure](../squad-onboard/references/storage.md#full-cloud-backup).
